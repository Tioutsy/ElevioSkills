# ELEVIO SKILLS Long-Term Catalogue Architecture (ELH-01 to ELH-136)

## 1. Executive Summary & Curriculum Design Principles

This document defines the complete long-term course catalogue for ELEVIO SKILLS, spanning **136 carefully designed courses** (`ELH-01` through `ELH-136`).

### Core Curriculum Rules
1. **Zero Generic Filler:** Every course delivers practical, professional workplace learning designed to empower employees to take defensible operational actions.
2. **Distinct Pedagogical Value:** No two courses substantially duplicate one another. Paired courses (e.g. foundational vs. specialist) are clearly differentiated by audience, scope, and technical depth.
3. **Multi-Layer Architecture:** Courses are categorized across 6 distinct relevance layers (Universal Core, Sector Core, Department Core, Role Specialist, Management & Leadership, and Advanced ESG Professional).
4. **Preservation of Existing 34 Production Courses:** `ELH-01` through `ELH-34` remain the foundational bedrock of the catalogue, maintaining their canonical titles, IDs, prerequisites, and learning objectives.
