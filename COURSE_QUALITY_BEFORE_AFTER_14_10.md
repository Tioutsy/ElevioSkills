## Key Strategic Enhancements by Phase

### Phase 1: Remediating the 8 Weak Courses (All Upgraded from $<70$ to $\ge 86$)
- **`ELH-03` Energy Efficiency (+20 pts):** Added 24°C central setpoint economics, daytime envelope air sealing, idle parasitic power protocols, and 10 scenario questions.
- **`ELH-04` Water Conservation (+21 pts):** Added continuous leak reporting workflows, washdown trigger nozzles, hygiene vs conservation boundaries, cooling tower sub-meters, and 10 scenario questions.
- **`ELH-06` Green Office Practices (+20 pts):** Restructured into *Office Management & Sustainable Workplace Administration Systems* (stationery amnesties, pull-printing, confidential document shredding, low-waste catering, green lease scheduling) with 10 questions.
- **`ELH-07` Carbon Footprint (+19 pts):** Added Activity Data $\times$ Emission Factor mechanics, Scope 1–3 boundaries, high-GWP refrigerant leak logs, anti-greenwash verification, and 10 questions.
- **`ELH-08` Biodiversity (+19 pts):** Added native/endemic/invasive distinctions, coral lagoon runoff prevention, downward-shielded night lighting for seabirds, Pause–Protect–Report–Record, and 10 questions.
- **`ELH-31` Social Responsibility (+20 pts):** Added core operations vs philanthropy distinctions, operator fatigue management, contractor welfare/modern slavery due diligence, psychological safety, and 10 questions.
- **`ELH-32` Ethics & Governance (+19 pts):** Added conflict of interest declaration registers, gift thresholds, backdating/forgery prevention, whistleblower safety, and 10 questions.
- **`ELH-33` ESG Data & Reporting (+21 pts):** Evolved into *ESG Measurement, Reporting & Disclosure Basics* covering financial vs double materiality, GRI, ISSB (IFRS S1/S2), and audit assurance.

### Phase 2: Role Course Interactivity (`ELH-24`, `ELH-25`, `ELH-27`, `ELH-28`)
- Added $\ge 3$ embedded `decision_scenario` blocks to each course.
- HR: Recruitment claims verification, competency matrix shift training access, positive engagement vs shaming.
- Finance: HVAC payback validation, solar PPA vs CAPEX purchase, utility milestone payment verification.
- Facilities: Contractor repair sign-off with bucket under pipe, thermal setpoints vs tenant comfort, overnight sub-meter anomalies.
- Marketing: Tender deadline green claims, ISO 14021 brochure compliance, enterprise ESG RFP questionnaires.

### Phase 3: Paired Course Differentiation
- **`ELH-05` vs `ELH-26`:** ELH-05 clearly established as *Sustainable Purchasing for Non-Specialists* (general requisitioners, need vs want, simple supplier questions), leaving ELH-26 as the advanced specialist procurement course.
- **`ELH-18` vs `ELH-33`:** ELH-18 focused on *Internal Operational Data Collection & Evidence* (SOURCE framework, meter logs, units, zero vs missing data), while ELH-33 focuses on *External ESG Measurement, Frameworks & Disclosures* (GRI, ISSB, materiality, assurance).
