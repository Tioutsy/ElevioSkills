# SPRINT 15 — COMPETENCY PROFICIENCY & EVIDENCE MODEL

## 1. The 5-Point Proficiency Scale

ELEVIO SKILLS defines employee sustainability capability across a standardized 5-point explainable scale:

| Level | Designation | Operational Definition & Observable Evidence |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **0** | **Not Yet Evidenced** | No verifiable learning history or assessment record in this competency domain. |
| **1** | **Awareness** | Understands core climate, environmental, or regulatory concepts (Universal Core completed). |
| **2** | **Working Knowledge** | Understands standard workplace operating procedures and compliance parameters (Applied Practice completed). |
| **3** | **Applied** | Can execute, troubleshoot, and supervise domain-specific workflows (Role Specialist / Mgmt completed). |
| **4** | **Advanced** | Masters strategic ESG governance, carbon accounting, or regulatory disclosures (Strategic / Masterclass completed). |

---

## 2. Evidence Hierarchy & Non-Punitive Rules

1. **Verifiable Learning Evidence:**
   - Completed Course Tier & Archetype (Universal &rarr; Applied &rarr; Specialist &rarr; Strategic).
   - Validated Quiz Performance ($\ge 80\%$ score with question rationale review).
   - Completed Prerequisite Sequences.
2. **Strict Non-Evidence Exclusions:**
   - Login frequency, platform streaks, gamification points, or leaderboard position are strictly excluded from competency calculations.
3. **Traceability Guarantee:**
   Every displayed proficiency level links directly to its underlying course completion records and assessment scores.
