# SPRINT 15.1 — COMPETENCY CALIBRATION SPECIFICATION

## 1. Executive Summary

This document specifies the calibrated competency calculation model, establishing strict defensibility rules for proficiency levels, course archetype caps, question-level mapping, and attempt history.

---

## 2. Calibrated Proficiency Derivation Rules

| Proficiency Level | Required Course Archetype | Minimum Validated Score | Assessment Weighting & Evidence Depth |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Level 0 (Not Evidenced)** | None | N/A | No learning evidence in this domain. |
| **Level 1 (Awareness)** | Foundation / Universal Core | $\ge 80\%$ | Recall & conceptual understanding questions. |
| **Level 2 (Working Knowledge)**| Applied Workplace Practice | $\ge 80\%$ | Scenario decision dilemmas & procedure workflows. |
| **Level 3 (Applied)** | Role Specialist / Supervisor | $\ge 80\%$ | Advanced domain troubleshooting & oversight scenarios. |
| **Level 4 (Advanced)** | Strategic Leadership / Capstone | $\ge 80\%$ | Multi-domain ESG strategy, CapEx, or audit governance. |

---

## 3. Key Calibration Safeguards

1. **Archetype Evidence Capping:** A 100% score on an introductory Foundation course (`ELH-01` or `ELH-02`) **caps strictly at Level 1 (Awareness)** and cannot independently establish Level 3 or 4 capability.
2. **Decoupling Company Priorities:** Corporate strategic priorities (e.g. `COMP_ENERGY`) increase recommendation urgency and TNA prioritization, but **do not artificially increment the employee's occupational proficiency target (+1)**.
3. **Attempt & Retry Handling:** First-attempt failures followed by successful retries are credited at standard proficiency with `MODERATE` confidence, avoiding punitive branding while retaining developmental audit trails.
