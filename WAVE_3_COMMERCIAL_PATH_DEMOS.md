# ELEVIO SKILLS Commercial Learning Path Demonstrations

This document presents polished, client-facing learning pathways across four core industries. Each profile illustrates how ELEVIO structures role-tailored sustainability training without revealing internal scoring logic.

---

## 1. Hospitality & Resort Operations

### A. Hotel Room Attendant (Frontline Individual Contributor)
- **Role Summary:** Frontline guest room cleaning, linen handling, and resource conservation.
- **Assigned Pathway (6 Courses / ~2.5 Hours):**
  1. `ELH-01`: Sustainability Foundations & Climate Literacy *(Core)*
  2. `ELH-02`: Water Stewardship & Practical Conservation *(Core)*
  3. `ELH-03`: Energy Efficiency & Workplace Decarbonization *(Core)*
  4. `ELH-35`: Sustainable Housekeeping & Chemical Safety *(Sector)*
  5. `ELH-36`: Linen Reuse Programs & Laundry Conservation *(Sector)*
  6. `ELH-12`: Capstone: Applied Workplace Sustainability Certification *(Capstone)*
- **Next Best Course:** `ELH-01`
- **Recommended Elective:** `ELH-40`: Resort Guest Engagement & Green Nudges

---

### B. Housekeeping Supervisor (Operational Team Leader)
- **Role Summary:** Shift supervision, linen quality audit, chemical inventory, and team training.
- **Assigned Pathway (9 Courses / ~3.8 Hours):**
  1. `ELH-01`..`04`, `ELH-34`: Core Sustainability Foundation
  2. `ELH-35`: Sustainable Housekeeping & Chemical Safety *(Sector)*
  3. `ELH-36`: Linen Reuse Programs & Laundry Conservation *(Sector)*
  4. `ELH-38`: Hotel Laundry & Water Stewardship *(Sector)*
  5. `ELH-13`: Sustainability Action Planning & Goal Setting *(Management)*
  6. `ELH-14`: Leading Green Teams & Employee Engagement *(Management)*
  7. `ELH-12`: Capstone Certification
- **Next Best Course:** `ELH-01`
- **Recommended Elective:** `ELH-119`: Engaging Frontline Employees in Green Initiatives

---

### C. Resort General Manager (Executive Leadership)
- **Role Summary:** Strategic asset governance, utility CapEx budgets, luxury brand standards.
- **Assigned Pathway (8 Courses / ~4.0 Hours):**
  1. `ELH-01`..`04`, `ELH-34`: Core Sustainability Foundation
