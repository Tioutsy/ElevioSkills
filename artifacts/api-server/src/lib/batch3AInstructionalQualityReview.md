## 3. Qualitative Pedagogical Reviews & Determinations

### Course `ELH-13`: Sustainability Action Planning
- **Determination**: `ACCEPTED`
- **Pedagogical Shift**: Moved from generic introductory statements to a structured 12-month departmental roadmap methodology with RACI ownership, Capex/Opex allocations, and Effort-vs-Impact matrices.
- **Scenario Rigor**: Features a budget allocation dilemma between high-yield IoT sub-metering versus marketing handbooks, followed by an off-track Q2 milestone supply chain contingency scenario.
- **Cognitive Depth**: 75% application/judgement items evaluating baseline metrics, RACI governance, and variance escalation.
- **Recommendation Alignment**: Recommends `ELH-14` (Setting Departmental Sustainability Goals, D3), ensuring coherent progressive flow.

### Course `ELH-14`: Setting Departmental Sustainability Goals
- **Determination**: `ACCEPTED`
- **Pedagogical Shift**: Deep focus on mathematical normalization (e.g. laundry water per occupied room, energy per tonne manufactured) to isolate true eco-efficiency during rapid business growth.
- **Scenario Rigor**: Real resort housekeeping laundry dilemma comparing flawed absolute targets with resilient intensity metrics.
- **Cognitive Depth**: Tests span-of-control principles, historical 12-month baseline data compilation, and employee appraisal alignment.
- **Recommendation Alignment**: Recommends `ELH-117` (Setting SMART Departmental Sustainability Targets, D3).

### Course `ELH-15`: Building a Workplace Sustainability Team
- **Determination**: `ACCEPTED`
- **Pedagogical Shift**: Concrete focus on cross-functional pillar assembly (Facilities, Finance, Operations, HR), formal chartering, and executive sponsorship.
- **Scenario Rigor**: Overcoming maintenance supervisor pushback by demonstrating operational pain-point alignment (chiller PM and air leaks).
- **Cognitive Depth**: Evaluates volunteer burnout mitigation, structured 45-minute sprint agendas, and distributed leadership succession.
- **Recommendation Alignment**: Recommends `ELH-22` (Creating and Running Effective Green Teams, D3).

### Course `ELH-128`: Sustainability for Health & Safety (HSE) Officers
- **Determination**: `ACCEPTED WITH MINOR CORRECTIONS`
- **Pedagogical Shift**: Integrating environmental risk matrices into OHS Hierarchy of Controls, GHS chemical labeling, and risk-assessed secondary containment sizing.
- **Scenario Rigor**: Industrial solvent drum puncture containment near storm drains and extreme heat stress intervention.
- **Cognitive Depth**: Tests tropical wet-bulb globe temperature (WBGT) physiology, SDS 30-second access, and unified HSE audit inspections.
- **Statutory Claim Correction**: Replaced the previous generic attribution of a universal statutory 110% bunding mandate with the precise legal standard: *“Secondary containment should be sized according to the substance, container capacity, site risk assessment, applicable legal requirements and approved technical guidance. A 110% capacity rule is commonly used in some standards and policies, but it must not be presented as a universal Mauritian statutory requirement without a directly applicable provision.”*
- **Recommendation Alignment**: Recommends `ELH-130` (Sustainability Communications & Green Claims, D3).
