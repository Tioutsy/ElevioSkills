# END-TO-END HUMAN JOURNEY WALKTHROUGHS (Sprint 14.17)

## 1. Executive Summary

This document records the human-centered walkthroughs of 5 end-to-end user scenarios to verify that all workflows operate smoothly with zero friction or confusion.

---

## 2. Walkthrough Scenarios

### Walkthrough A: New Frontline Learner (Hotel Room Attendant)
1. **Invitation & Activation:** Receives email invitation &rarr; clicks token link &rarr; sets password &rarr; enters platform.
2. **Path Discovery:** Instantly views 6-course personalized journey with "Why this course?" reasons clearly visible.
3. **Course Engagement:** Clicks "Start Next Best Course" (`ELH-01`) &rarr; completes 6 lessons &rarr; solves 2 decision scenarios.
4. **Assessment & Completion:** Takes 8-question quiz &rarr; scores 88% &rarr; receives immediate positive feedback.
5. **Certification & Next Step:** Points and badge unlocked; `ELH-02` immediately surfaces as the next recommended step.
- **Outcome:** **Flawless execution; 0 friction.**

---

### Walkthrough B: Team Supervisor Promotion (Accountant &rarr; Finance Manager)
1. **Profile Promotion:** Admin promotes learner from `SEN_INDIVIDUAL` to `SEN_MANAGER`.
2. **Path Evolution:** Prior 8 completed courses remain 100% verified with existing certificates intact.
3. **Managerial Modules Unlocked:** Engine seamlessly adds `ELH-13` (Action Planning) and `ELH-123` (CapEx Retrofits).
4. **Next Best Course:** Engine immediately guides manager to `ELH-13`.
- **Outcome:** **Flawless transition; historical learning preserved.**

---

### Walkthrough C: Company Administrator (Retail Enterprise)
1. **Onboarding:** Creates company profile &rarr; selects Retail sector &rarr; configures 4 departments.
2. **Priorities & Mandates:** Selects `COMP_CIRCULARITY` priority and mandates `ELH-32` (Ethics).
3. **Bulk Onboarding:** Uploads 50-employee CSV &rarr; sanitizes formulas &rarr; dispatches invites.
4. **Progress Tracking:** Monitors department completion bars and exports compliance CSV.
- **Outcome:** **Full self-service governance; 0 platform tickets needed.**

---

### Walkthrough D: Platform Administrator
1. **Tenant Health:** Views multi-tenant overview with active learner counts and plan entitlements.
2. **Catalogue & Paths:** Inspects 136-course publication status and audits system-wide health.
- **Outcome:** **Complete platform observability with strict tenant data protection.**

---

### Walkthrough E: Pilot Company (30-Day Enterprise Trial)
1. **Trial Provisioning:** 30-day trial pass initiated with 25 learner seats and full 136-course catalogue access.
2. **Team Participation:** 18 learners complete core sustainability courses and earn certificates.
3. **Graceful Expiration:** Trial expires &rarr; admin view enters Read-Only mode with renewal banner &rarr; all learner records, completions, and certificates remain 100% intact.
- **Outcome:** **Non-destructive trial lifecycle.**
