# SPRINT 15.2 — TEST-OUT GOVERNANCE & MANDATORY PROTECTION

## 1. Executive Summary

This policy establishes strict boundaries separating *Elective Learning Path Personalization* from *Mandatory Compliance Training Requirements*.

---

## 2. Test-Out Policy Architecture

| Learning Track | Diagnostic Impact Policy | Default System Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Recommended Elective Courses** | Strong baseline evidence deprioritizes unnecessary introductory modules. | **ALLOWED** (Adaptive efficiency) |
| **Optional Expansion Modules** | High-level baseline results elevate advanced specialist topics. | **ALLOWED** (Personalized growth) |
| **Mandatory Compliance Assignments** | **NO AUTOMATIC TEST-OUT.** Diagnostic does not satisfy mandatory internal assignments. | **`TEST_OUT_ALLOWED = FALSE`** (Protected) |

---

## 3. Audit & Compliance Protections

1. **Zero Silent Course Completions:** Passing a diagnostic does NOT mark any course as completed or issue a course completion certificate.
2. **Immutable Exemption Logging:** If an enterprise client administrator explicitly overrides a compliance track for a verified employee, a cryptographic audit log records the authorizing admin, timestamp, and diagnostic evidence ID.
