# SPRINT 15.2.1 — DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION BANK STANDARD

## 1. Executive Summary

This standard establishes the pedagogical, taxonomic, and difficulty benchmarks governing the standalone diagnostic question bank for ELEVIO SKILLS.

---

## 2. Question Taxonomy & Quality Requirements

Every diagnostic item must include the following metadata:
- **`id`:** Unique canonical identifier (e.g. `DIAG-ENG-02A`).
- **`competency`:** Primary canonical competency domain.
- **`subcompetency`:** Granular subcompetency tag (e.g. `SUB_ENERGY_EFFICIENCY`).
- **`difficulty`:** Calibrated difficulty band (`D1` Basic, `D2` Working Knowledge, `D3` Applied, `D4` Strategic).
- **`evidenceType`:** Evidence depth (`UNDERSTANDING`, `APPLICATION`, `SCENARIO / JUDGEMENT`).
- **`equivalentGroupId`:** Cluster ID (e.g. `GRP_ENG_D2_HVAC`) enabling baseline/reassessment separation.
- **`rationale`:** Clear operational explanation of the correct choice.

---

## 3. Evidence Distribution Benchmarks

- **Understanding (D1):** $\approx 25\%$ (Conceptual fundamentals, scope definitions).
- **Application (D2):** $\approx 40\%$ (Operational decisions, troubleshooting, procedural execution).
- **Scenario / Judgement (D3 & D4):** $\approx 35\%$ (Multi-variable capital retrofits, ESG assurance, strategic trade-offs).
- **High Confidence Rule:** High confidence strictly requires multiple independent items across diverse equivalent groups. Single items can never yield High confidence.
