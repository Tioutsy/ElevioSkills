## 3. Question Bank Quality Standard

1. **Course Independence:** Questions are formulated to be answerable from existing workplace experience without requiring the candidate to have read a specific ELEVIO lesson immediately prior.
2. **Defensible Answer Keys:** All items feature unambiguous correct keys supported by empirical operational rationales.
3. **Catalogue Freeze Protection:** Diagnostic items are stored in dedicated diagnostic banks and **do not modify or expand the 136-course library**.
