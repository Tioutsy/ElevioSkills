# WAVE 3 SECTOR, DEPARTMENT & COMPETENCY COVERAGE GAP ANALYSIS

## 1. Executive Summary

As of the conclusion of Sprint 14.14, ELEVIO SKILLS features **72 active production courses** out of the 136-course long-term architecture (53% complete).

This comprehensive audit evaluates the remaining 64 unproduced courses across all 10 canonical sectors, 15 sub-sectors, 12 departments, 5 seniority tiers, and 10 competency ladders to select **28 high-impact Wave 3 production courses**.
