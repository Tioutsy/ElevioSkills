# ELEVIO SKILLS Long-Term Catalogue Production Roadmap

## 1. Executive Summary & Prioritization Strategy

To expand ELEVIO SKILLS from the 34 existing production courses to the full 136-course long-term catalogue without compromising instructional rigor or overloading authoring capacity, courses are prioritized into **Four Priority Tiers (P0–P3)** and scheduled across **Five Structured Production Waves**.

```
Production Waves Architecture:
├── Existing Production Baseline: 34 Courses (ELH-01 to ELH-34) [ACTIVE]
├── Wave 1: Management & Core Cross-Sector Essentials (18 Courses)
├── Wave 2: Hospitality, Property & Corporate Real Estate (22 Courses)
├── Wave 3: Manufacturing, Retail & Logistics Operations (24 Courses)
├── Wave 4: Banking, Finance & Advanced ESG Professional (20 Courses)
└── Wave 5: Agriculture, Healthcare & Specialist Depth (18 Courses)
```

---

## 2. Priority Ranking Matrix (P0 – P3)

Every future course is ranked based on **Cross-Sector Value ($V$)** and **Immediate Commercial Market Demand ($D$)**:

$$\text{Priority Score} = V_{\text{breadth}} \times D_{\text{market}}$$

| Priority Tier | Definition & Strategic Purpose | Course Count | Criteria for Inclusion |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **P0: High Value / Cross-Sector Core** | Essential modules needed across multiple sectors or large employee populations. | **38 courses** | High commercial demand in Mauritius; unlocks core pathways for multiple departments. |
| **P1: Priority Sector / Role Core** | Deep industry-specific modules essential for primary target sectors. | **42 courses** | Critical for Hospitality, Real Estate, Retail, Manufacturing, and Banking sectors. |
| **P2: Specialist & Operational Depth**| Niche technical or functional depth modules. | **22 courses** | Enhances existing pathways with technical or advanced operational rigor. |
| **P3: Long-Term & Emerging Topics** | Progressive or emerging sustainability sub-disciplines. | **0 courses** | *Deferred to future catalogue extensions beyond ELH-136.* |
