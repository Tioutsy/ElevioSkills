# ELEVIO SKILLS Commercial Catalogue Summary

## 1. Product Overview

**ELEVIO SKILLS** is an enterprise workforce sustainability and ESG learning platform engineered to deliver role-tailored, compliance-grade education across all organizational tiers.

Unlike generic corporate e-learning libraries that present static catalogs, ELEVIO uses an **Intelligent Learning Path Engine** to map exact employee responsibilities, industry standards, and company priorities into bite-sized, actionable learning journeys.

## 3. The 4-Tier Learning Journey Framework

1. **Core Sustainability Foundation:** Universal climate, water, energy, and circularity literacy for every employee.
2. **Your Sector:** Industry-specific operating standards, compliance mandates, and resource efficiency practices.
3. **Your Role:** Departmental and technical job-specific standard operating procedures (SOPs).
4. **Management & Strategic Leadership:** Action planning, team engagement, CapEx financial modeling, and board ESG governance.

---

## 4. International Standards & Legal Rigor

ELEVIO courses are built in alignment with major global reporting frameworks and regulatory standards:
- **Corporate Reporting:** CSRD / ESRS, ISSB / IFRS S1 & S2, GRI Standards, SEC Climate Disclosures.
- **Climate & Decarbonization:** GHG Protocol Corporate Standard (Scopes 1, 2, and 3), SBTi (Science Based Targets initiative).
- **Risk & Nature:** TCFD, TNFD LEAP Methodology.
- **Industry & Quality Standards:** ISO 14001 (Environmental), ISO 50001 (Energy), ISO 45001 (HSE), ISO 20121 (Events), LEED, BREEAM.
