# ELEVIO SKILLS — LEARNER QUICK-START GUIDE

Welcome to **ELEVIO SKILLS**! You have been enrolled in a personalized sustainability learning journey tailored to your specific role and company.

---

## 1. Getting Started in 3 Simple Steps

1. **Open Your Invite:** Click the activation link sent to your work email and set your password.
2. **View Your Learning Path:** Your dashboard displays your tailored learning journey divided into clear sections:
   - **Core Sustainability:** Essential foundations for every team member.
   - **Your Sector & Role:** Practical actions directly applicable to your daily job.
   - **Leadership & Growth:** Skills to lead green initiatives at work.
3. **Start Your First Course:** Look for the highlighted **Next Best Course** banner and click **Start Learning**.

---

## 2. How Courses Work

- **Bite-Sized Modules:** Each course takes approximately **20 to 25 minutes** to complete.
- **Workplace Scenarios:** Test your judgment with interactive scenarios reflecting real-world workplace decisions.
- **Passing the Quiz:** Score **80% or higher** on the final 8-to-10 question quiz to earn course points. If you don't pass on your first try, you can review the lesson material and retake the quiz immediately!
- **Earn Verified Certificates:** Completing required courses unlocks official, verifiable digital certificates and badges to celebrate your sustainability achievements.

Happy Learning!
