# SPRINT 15.2 — MEASURED LEARNING IMPACT MODEL

## 1. Executive Summary

The ELEVIO Measured Learning Impact model enables organizations to quantify capability progression by evaluating employee competency proficiencies and evidence confidence Before vs After training.

---

## 2. Impact States & Defensible Definitions

| Impact State | Concrete Evidence Transition | Defensible Reporting Language |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PROFICIENCY_INCREASED** | Demonstrated $\ge +1$ proficiency growth ($0 \rightarrow 1$, $1 \rightarrow 2$, $2 \rightarrow 3$). | "Learning evidence increased from Awareness to Working Knowledge." |
| **EVIDENCE_STRENGTHENED** | Same proficiency level, but confidence elevated from `LOW` to `HIGH`. | "Capability confirmed at Working Knowledge with multi-source evidence." |
| **GAP_CLOSED** | Current proficiency reached or exceeded target proficiency. | "Role development gap successfully closed through verified coursework." |
| **MAINTAINED** | Baseline capability preserved without observed degradation. | "Competency maintained at certified baseline." |
| **MORE_EVIDENCE_NEEDED** | Conflicting or preliminary results following reassessment. | "Additional workplace learning recommended to confirm capability." |

---

## 3. Responsible Analytics Policy

- **No False Business Extrapolations:** ELEVIO reports verified *learning capability deltas*, strictly avoiding ungrounded claims regarding direct monetary ROI, exact carbon tonnages, or external regulatory certifications unless separately audited.
