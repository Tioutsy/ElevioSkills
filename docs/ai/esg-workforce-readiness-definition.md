# ESG Workforce Readiness Indicator — Scope & Operational Definition

## Overview
The ESG Workforce Readiness Indicator measures an organisation's workforce preparation and knowledge exposure across key Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) topics based strictly on ELEVIO SKILLS learning and assessment evidence.

---

## What the Indicator Measures
- **Assessment Evidence Coverage**: The proportion of active employees with completed quiz and scenario assessment records.
- **Demonstrated Knowledge**: Average scores and topic mastery ratios achieved on validated knowledge assessments.
- **Priority Topic Exposure**: Workforce alignment with company-declared ESG training priorities (e.g., Waste Management, Carbon Accounting, Supply Chain Governance).
- **Departmental Participation**: The spread and depth of learning engagement across operational units (Facilities, HR, Operations, Finance, Sales).
- **Knowledge Gap Severity**: Penalties for critical assessment failures or unaddressed operational skill deficits.

---

## What the Indicator Does NOT Measure
To prevent false precision or misleading claims, the Readiness Indicator explicitly **EXCLUDES**:
- Actual corporate greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, Scope 1/2/3 data, or energy reduction metrics.
- External regulatory compliance status (CSRD, SEC, ISSB, NFRD, TCFD).
- Physical workplace behavior, operational safety compliance, or environmental audit results.
- Third-party ESG ratings or sustainability index scores.
- Certified individual professional competence or legally binding qualification.
