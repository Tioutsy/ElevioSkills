# ELEVIO Skills — Visual Learning Standard

## Core Principle

> **"When understanding the physical context materially affects the learner's judgement, provide visual context before requiring a decision."**

ELEVIO Skills training is designed to build practical workplace judgement rather than abstract theoretical memorisation. Visual assets must serve a clear pedagogical role in helping learners observe, evaluate, and act upon realistic operational scenarios.

---

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Classification Categories

Every visual asset introduced into ELEVIO courses, micro-lessons, and product experiences must be classified into one of three strict categories:

### Category A: INSTRUCTIONAL VISUAL (Preferred)
* **Definition**: A visual that depicts a physical situation, workspace environment, equipment state, operational process, or consequence necessary for the learner's understanding and decision-making.
* **Role**: Primary learning medium that establishes the scene before the learner evaluates choices.
* **Requirements**:
  - Photorealistic and authentic to contemporary corporate environments (e.g., Mauritian office spaces, logistics hubs, hospitality venues).
  - 16:9 aspect ratio for standard scenario headers.
  - Zero embedded typography, watermarks, or staged symbolic clipart.
  - Meaningful, descriptive `alt` text provided for accessibility.
  - Written scenario text remains authoritative and accessible so that visual context reinforces rather than gatekeeps factual comprehension.
* **Example**: Screen 2 of *Experience ELEVIO* — A realistic 16:9 photograph of a vacant boardroom with air conditioning running, lights on, uncollected confidential slides, and disposable cups on the conference table.

---

### Category B: SUPPORTING VISUAL (Optional)
* **Definition**: A visual that helps establish secondary context, role recognition, or interface preview without being strictly necessary to answer a question.
* **Role**: Reinforces comprehension and engagement without driving the core evaluation.
* **Requirements**:
  - Clean, restrained presentation consistent with the ELEVIO design system.
  - Clearly labelled if depicting sample/demonstration data.
  - Lightweight and responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile form factors.
* **Example**: Controlled UI previews of learner certificates and company management dashboards in *Experience ELEVIO* Screens 6 & 7.

---

### Category C: DECORATIVE VISUAL (Generally Avoid)
* **Definition**: An asset that adds visual flourish, abstract imagery, or generic environmental symbolism without improving pedagogical clarity or operational understanding.
* **Role**: Prohibited or minimized.
* **Guidance**:
  - Avoid generic stock photos of green leaves, globe illustrations, lightbulb diagrams, or cartoon mascots.
  - Do not introduce visual clutter that distracts from practical workplace decisions.

---

## 2. Asset Standards & Production Guidelines

1. **Aspect Ratio & Framing**:
   - Standard Scenario Visuals: `16:9` widescreen.
   - Course Thumbnails: `16:9` with focal points centered to prevent cropping distortion on mobile viewports.
2. **Format & Optimisation**:
   - Web-optimized JPEG or WebP assets with minimal footprint (< 250 KB target for standard scenario photography).
   - High visual fidelity without compression artifacts.
3. **Accessibility**:
   - All visual elements must include concise, accurate `alt` text explaining the observable scenario elements.
   - Color contrast standards (WCAG 2.1 AA) must be preserved for all overlaid badges and surrounding UI controls.
4. **Pedagogical Independence**:
   - Written scenario text must remain self-sufficient so screen-reader users and text-only learners receive identical learning outcomes.

---

## 3. Implementation Scope

* **Current Implementation**: Screen 2 of the *Experience ELEVIO* (`/experience`) autonomous product proof.
* **Catalogue Application**: Future course maintenance sprints will audit existing canonical courses to introduce Category A Instructional Visuals where physical context directly enhances learner decision-making.
