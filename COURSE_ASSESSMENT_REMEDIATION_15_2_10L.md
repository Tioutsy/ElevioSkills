1. **CSRD / ESRS (`ELH-132`)**: Directive 2022/2464/EU, ESRS 1 chapter 3 (Double Materiality OR rule), ESRS 2 cross-cutting mandates, ESRS E1-6 (Scope 2 dual reporting), ISAE 3000 / ISSA 5000 limited-to-reasonable assurance path, ESEF/XHTML XBRL digital tagging.

3. **TNFD Framework (`ELH-134`)**: TNFD Recommendations v1.0, LEAP assessment approach (Locate, Evaluate, Assess, Prepare), Natural Capital Protocol dependency/impact pathways, Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (GBF) 2030/2050 targets.
4. **Internal Carbon Pricing (`ELH-135`)**: World Bank/CPLC guidelines, CapEx shadow hurdle rate discount cash flow adjustments, internal fee revenue recycling into green transition funds, Marginal Abatement Cost Curve (MACC) calibration.
5. **SBTi Net-Zero Standard (`ELH-136`)**: SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard v1.2, 1.5°C near-term (5–10 years, 4.2% annual linear reduction), 90–95% long-term value chain abatement by 2050, Beyond Value Chain Mitigation (BVCM) separation, permanent Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR) neutralization for residual emissions.
