# SPRINT 15.2 — ADAPTIVE DIAGNOSTIC & STOPPING RULES

## 1. Executive Summary

This document specifies the deterministic branching and stopping rules governing diagnostic sessions, ensuring concise, accurate assessment without fatigue.

---

## 2. Adaptive Branching Rules

1. **Initial Entry:** Session begins with a Level 1 (Understanding) or Level 2 (Application) item.
2. **Correct Response ($\ge 1$):** Stepping rule promotes the learner to test a Level 2 or Level 3 scenario question in that competency.
3. **Incorrect Response ($0$):** Branching maintains baseline testing to gather confidence on Level 1 awareness.
4. **Stopping Rule:**
   - **Minimum Evidence:** At least 2 questions attempted per tested competency.
   - **Maximum Bound:** Session terminates after 3 questions per competency (or 9 total across the diagnostic) to eliminate fatigue.
   - **Confidence Cutoff:** High confidence stops further questioning in that domain immediately.
