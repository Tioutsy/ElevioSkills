# LEGAL & PRIVACY READINESS REPORT (Release 1.0)

## 1. Executive Summary

This report audits the personal data inventory, processing purposes, data minimization, and privacy compliance posture for ELEVIO SKILLS under Mauritian Data Protection Act (DPA 2017) and EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards.

## 3. Data Minimization & Retention Governance

1. **Data Minimization:** No personal home addresses, national identity numbers, personal financial payment cards, or sensitive medical health records are collected or processed.
2. **Data Retention Policy:**
   - **Active Learners:** Retained for duration of corporate subscription.
   - **Deactivated Learners:** Retained in read-only audit state for 5 years to support corporate ESG compliance audits (ISAE 3000 / CSRD).
   - **Expired Trials / Cancelled Tenants:** Company accounts set to Read-Only; purged upon formal written customer request after 90 days.
3. **Right to Erasure / Deletion Workflow:**
   - Platform Administrators possess an audited data-anonymization script that scrubs learner name and email while preserving aggregated, anonymous company progress totals.
