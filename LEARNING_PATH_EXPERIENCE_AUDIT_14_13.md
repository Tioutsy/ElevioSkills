# ELEVIO SKILLS Learning Path Experience Audit (Sprint 14.13)

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

This audit evaluates the **real learner experience** generated by the Intelligent Learning Path Engine across 22 canonical workplace personas.

### Evaluation Criteria & Dimensions (100-Point Rubric)
- **Relevance (25 pts):** Direct alignment with daily operational tasks and responsibilities.
- **Role Fit (15 pts):** Matches seniority, job family, and departmental scope.
- **Sequence (15 pts):** Logical progression: Foundations &rarr; Sector &rarr; Role &rarr; Management &rarr; Capstone.
- **Duplication Control (10 pts):** Absence of redundant or overlapping modules.
- **Training Load (10 pts):** Adherence to bounded path length guardrails (4–8 Frontline, 6–10 Specialist/Supervisor, 8–12 Manager).
- **Progression (10 pts):** Increasing managerial and strategic depth with seniority.
- **Explainability (10 pts):** Clear, non-technical "Why this course?" reasons.
- **Commercial Credibility (5 pts):** Trustworthy, tailored curriculum suitable for enterprise HR buyers.
