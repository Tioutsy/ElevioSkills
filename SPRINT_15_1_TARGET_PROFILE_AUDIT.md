# SPRINT 15.1 — ROLE TARGET PROFILE AUDIT & CALIBRATION

## 1. Executive Summary

This audit evaluates target competency profiles across 22 canonical organizational personas to verify that occupational targets are realistic, calibrated by seniority, and free from over-targeting or under-targeting anomalies.

---

| Seniority Tier | Representative Job Titles | Target Proficiency Range | Over/Under-Targeting Finding | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: |

| **Specialist / Supervisor** | Accountant, Procurement Officer, Engineer| **Level 2 to Level 3** | Calibrated; role domain requires Level 3. | **PASS** |

---

## 3. Decoupling Company Strategic Priorities

- **Previous Sprint 15 Risk:** Company priorities previously added an artificial global `+1` to every employee's proficiency target, distorting occupational requirements.
- **Sprint 15.1 Calibrated Resolution:** Company priorities now boost **recommendation urgency**, **Next Best Course weighting**, and **Training Needs Analysis ranking**, while preserving true occupational capability targets.
