# ELEVIO SKILLS — COMPANY ADMIN QUICK-START GUIDE

Welcome to **ELEVIO SKILLS**. As a Company Administrator, you have full control over your organization's sustainability learning programs. This guide outlines everything you need to know to get your team learning.

---

## 1. Setting Up Your Organization (5 Minutes)

1. **Log In:** Access your admin dashboard at the link provided in your welcome email.
2. **Confirm Sector:** Verify that your primary industry sector (e.g., Hospitality, Manufacturing, Banking) is correctly set.
3. **Configure Departments:** Review and add any internal departments (e.g. Operations, Housekeeping, Finance).
4. **Select Company Priorities:** Choose up to 4 sustainability focus areas (such as Energy Conservation or Water Stewardship). This automatically boosts relevant elective courses for your team.

---

## 2. Inviting Your Employees

1. **Prepare Your Roster:** Create a spreadsheet or CSV file with the following columns:
   - `First Name`
   - `Last Name`
   - `Email`
   - `Department`
   - `Job Title`
2. **Upload & Send:** Navigate to **Employees &rarr; Add Employees**, upload your CSV, and click **Send Invitations**.
3. **Automatic Personalization:** The moment an employee logs in, ELEVIO instantly generates a tailored 6-to-10 course learning journey built specifically for their job.

---

## 3. Managing Compliance & Tracking Progress

- **Mandatory Training:** Want everyone to complete Corporate Ethics (`ELH-32`)? Go to **Settings &rarr; Mandatory Courses** to enforce it company-wide.
- **Monitoring Completion:** Your **Dashboard** displays live progress bars, active learners, and department completion rates.
- **Auditing & Certificates:** When external sustainability auditors request training evidence, click **Export Compliance Report** to download an audit-ready CSV.
